# Landscape — research backlog

These are candidate comparators, not verified rankings or claims of a market gap. Review current documentation, licenses and behavior before deciding positioning.

- Personal context and agent memory: [gbrain](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain), [mem0](https://github.com/mem0ai/mem0).
- Personal timelines and data aggregation: [Timelinize](https://github.com/timelinize/timelinize), [CFV](https://github.com/tunapro1234/central-finite-vault).
- Personal digital twins: [Second Me](https://github.com/mindverse/Second-Me), [WeClone](https://github.com/xming521/WeClone).
- Population life-event modeling: [life2vec paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-023-00573-5). Its population-level research question is distinct from a personal assistant.

For each, check supported sources, local/cloud boundaries, agent interfaces, evidence and provenance, evaluation, licenses, maintenance and onboarding. Test actual workflows rather than inferring features or adoption from a README. Identify a gap only after comparison; no exclusivity claim is established.
